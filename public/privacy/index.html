Privacy policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Sudoku is a small game. We collect only the information needed to run the game, keep it reliable, and save games when you choose to use an account.

Information we process

  • Game information, such as puzzle state, moves, notes, elapsed time, and completion status.
  • Basic technical records needed for security and reliability, such as request time, error details, and network information recorded by the hosting service.
  • If you choose Google sign-in, your Google account identifier, email address, and display name. We do not receive your Google password or access to your email, files, contacts, or other Google services.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide sign-in, associate saved games with the correct account, restore gameplay, protect the service, and diagnose problems. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Browser storage and cookies

The game may use browser storage for display preferences and temporary guest-game state. If you sign in, the service uses a secure session cookie so your browser can access your account. You can remove local browser data through your browser settings.

Retention and deletion

Guest data is temporary. Account and saved-game data is retained while needed to provide the service or until it is deleted. Short-lived login transactions and expired sessions are discarded. You may request deletion of your account and associated saved-game data through the contact method provided with the site.

Sharing and security

Information is shared only with service providers required to host and operate the game, or when required by law. We use encrypted transport, limited identity scopes, revocable sessions, and access controls, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
